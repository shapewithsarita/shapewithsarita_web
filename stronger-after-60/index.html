Questions

Who is this book for?

It is for adults over 60 who want to stay strong and independent, and for sons, daughters and caregivers who want to help a parent. It starts with seated and supported exercises, so you do not need any experience.

Do I need a gym or equipment?

No. You need a sturdy chair without wheels and a fixed kitchen counter or railing. A light resistance band and two 500 ml water bottles are optional.

Is it safe if I have health conditions?

The book begins with a 2-minute readiness check and clear stop signs. It is general education, not medical advice, so please speak to your doctor before starting, especially with heart, bone, balance, kidney or neurological conditions, diabetes or high blood pressure.
