The short answer

  • Stronger After 60 is a 46-page illustrated Indian handbook of yoga, strength, balance and nutrition for healthy aging.
  • It has 33 exercises with pictures and videos, a 12-week plan, an Indian food guide, safety checklists and trackers.
  • ₹499, with a free 14-day nutrition plan.

After 60, the goal is not to look like an athlete. It is to climb stairs without fear, stand up from a chair easily, carry your own shopping, play with your grandchildren and enjoy meals that nourish you. I wrote Stronger After 60 to give Indian seniors and their families one practical place to start. This is a tour of what is inside, so you can decide if it is right for you or your parents.

Who is it for?

  • Seniors who want to stay strong and independent, whatever their starting point.
  • Sons, daughters and caregivers helping a parent move and eat better, including those who live far away.
  • People who cannot stand safely. Seated and bed-based exercises are included.

The 12-week plan

  • Weekly schedule: strength days, walking or seated cardio, balance practice, a light day and a recovery day, with exercise numbers to follow.
  • Progress over 12 weeks: learn technique, build the habit, progress gradually, then consolidate.
  • A 10-minute starter day for days when a full session feels like too much.

The Indian food guide

  • How much energy and protein you need, with tables by body weight.
  • Protein in common Indian foods, and tips for vegetarians.
  • The Indian Senior Plate and a hands-and-katori portion guide.
  • Fibre, vitamins and minerals, hydration, smart cooking and label tips.
  • General guidance for diabetes, high blood pressure, heart disease, kidney disease, bones, anaemia, low appetite and chewing difficulty.

Safety comes first

The book opens with a 2-minute readiness check and a clear message: read the safety chapter before you try any exercise. It explains when to speak to a doctor, how to set up a safe space and when to stop and seek help. It does not claim to cure disease, and it is general education, not medical advice.

Sarita's tip 🌿

Start with the readiness check and the 10-minute starter day. If you do only those for two weeks, you have already built the habit.

Frequently asked questions

What exactly do I get for ₹499?

A 46-page illustrated PDF handbook with 33 exercises, a 12-week plan, an Indian nutrition guide, safety and fall-proofing checklists and trackers. It also includes your free 14-day nutrition plan with a Day 7 and Day 14 assessment, and details of a free introductory call.

Do I need a gym or special equipment?

No. You need a sturdy chair without wheels and a fixed kitchen counter or railing. A light resistance band and two 500 ml water bottles are optional.

Is it safe for people with health conditions?

The book starts with a 2-minute readiness check, clear stop signs and an advice to speak to your doctor first. It is general education, not medical advice, so please follow your own doctor's advice.

Is it a printed book or a PDF?

It is a digital PDF handbook that you can read on a phone, tablet or computer. The trackers and checklists in Appendix A are designed to be printed, photocopied or copied into a notebook.

How do I receive the book after paying?

Fill in the short form on the book page. Sarita messages you on WhatsApp with a secure payment link. After payment is confirmed, you receive a download link on WhatsApp and email.

Is it for seniors or for their children?

Both. Seniors can read it themselves, and sons, daughters and caregivers can use it to help a parent. Parents who cannot stand safely can start with the seated and bed-based exercises.
