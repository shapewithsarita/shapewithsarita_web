Senior nutrition

How Much Protein Do Senior Citizens Need? Best Indian Sources

Sarita NawarCertified Nutrition & Fitness Coach
8 min readUpdated 13 Nov 2026

The short answer

  • Many healthy older adults need about 1.0 to 1.2 g of protein per kg of body weight per day. A 60 kg senior needs roughly 60 to 72 g.
  • Spread it across meals and snacks, with a protein food every time you eat.
  • Use everyday Indian foods first. People with kidney disease must follow their doctor's advice.

"At my age, I should eat less." Many seniors and families believe this, but after 60 your body actually needs good protein to protect muscle, support recovery and stay independent. This guide explains how much protein senior citizens need, how to reach it with Indian food and what to watch for.

Why protein matters after 60

  • It helps maintain and repair muscle, which supports strength and balance.
  • It supports immunity and recovery after illness.
  • It works best together with exercise, such as gentle strength training.
  • Poor protein intake can add to muscle loss as we age.

How much protein do you need?

The standard adult guideline of about 0.8 g per kg is set as a minimum to prevent deficiency. For older adults, the PROT-AGE and ESPEN expert groups suggest at least 1.0 to 1.2 g per kg per day for healthy older people. Those who exercise often need toward the upper end, and people with illness may need more, around 1.2 to 1.5 g per kg, under medical advice. People with severe kidney disease may need to limit protein.

To work out your own range, multiply your weight in kg by 1.0 and by 1.2. For example, 65 kg gives 65 to 78 g a day.

Vegetarian and vegan tips

  • Combine cereals with pulses across the day: dal-roti, rajma-rice, khichdi, idli-sambar.
  • Include milk, curd, paneer or tofu daily if you eat dairy or soya.
  • Add sprouts, roasted chana and peanuts as snacks.
  • Ask your doctor about vitamin B12. It is found in animal foods and fortified foods, and supplements may be advised.

If appetite is low or chewing is hard

  • Choose soft protein foods: khichdi, dal, curd, paneer bhurji, egg bhurji, soft fish curry and milk-based dishes.
  • Eat smaller, more frequent meals.
  • Ask a dentist or doctor to check chewing or swallowing problems.
  • If your parent is losing weight without trying, see a doctor. Do not aim to lose weight.

Do seniors need protein powder?

Start with food. India's dietary guidelines (ICMR-NIN, 2024) emphasise getting protein from everyday food combinations. If your appetite is poor or you cannot eat enough, ask your doctor or dietitian before using any supplement, especially with kidney disease.

Sarita's tip 🌿

Do not try to change everything at once. Just add one protein food to each meal and snack, like a katori of curd with lunch or a handful of roasted chana in the evening.

Check with your doctor first if

  • You have kidney disease or reduced kidney function.
  • You have liver, heart or other serious health conditions.
  • You are on a medical diet or have been told to limit fluids.
  • You have lost weight without trying or have a poor appetite.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Eating most protein only at dinner.
  • Relying on powders instead of real food.
  • Increasing protein suddenly, or without drinking enough fluids.
  • Eating dal and roti alone, without extra protein.
  • Skipping exercise, which helps muscles use protein.

For sons and daughters

Frequently asked questions

How much protein does a 70-year-old need per day?

Many expert groups suggest at least 1.0 to 1.2 g of protein per kg of body weight per day for healthy older adults. For a 70 kg person that is about 70 to 84 g a day. Needs can be higher with illness or exercise, and lower with severe kidney disease, so ask your doctor.

Is too much protein bad for the kidneys?

In people with healthy kidneys, protein within the recommended range is generally considered safe. People with kidney disease may need to limit protein and should follow a doctor's or renal dietitian's plan. Do not increase protein suddenly if you have kidney problems.

What are the best vegetarian protein sources in India?

Dal, rajma, chana, moong, sprouts, soya chunks, tofu, paneer, milk and curd. Combining cereals with pulses, like dal-roti, khichdi and idli-sambar, improves the overall protein quality across the day.

Can dal and roti alone give enough protein?

Usually not for older adults. One katori of dal gives about 6 to 7 g and a roti about 3 g. Add curd, milk, sprouts, paneer, tofu, soya or eggs so each meal has a good protein source.

Do seniors need protein powder?

Food comes first. Indian guidelines advise getting protein from everyday foods, and supplements are not a shortcut for muscle. If your appetite is poor or you cannot eat enough, ask your doctor or dietitian before using any supplement.

How should I spread protein through the day?

Include a protein food at every meal and snack, such as curd or egg at breakfast, dal and sprouts at lunch, paneer or soya at dinner. Experts suggest roughly 25 to 30 g per main meal for many older adults, adjusted to your size and appetite.
