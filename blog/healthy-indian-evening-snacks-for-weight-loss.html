Healthy Indian Evening Snacks for Women Who Want to Manage Their Weight

Sarita NawarCertified Nutrition & Fitness Coach
7 min readUpdated 22 Oct 2026

The short answer

  • Choose healthy Indian evening snacks with protein and fibre, like roasted chana, sprouts chaat, makhana or dhokla.
  • Keep portions to about a small bowl or one handful, and plan the snack before the 4 to 6 pm slump.
  • Pair your chai with a snack, and reduce the sugar slowly.

For many women, the evening is where a good day falls apart. Lunch was hours ago, you are tired after work or housework, the chai is ready, and the biscuits and namkeen are right there. The answer is not to skip snacks. It is to plan one that satisfies you. This guide gives you simple Indian evening snacks for women who want to manage their weight, with portions, chai tips and quick prep.

Why evening cravings happen

  • A long gap between lunch and dinner.
  • A lunch with too little protein, so you are hungry again by 4 pm.
  • Tiredness and stress, which make quick, sweet or fried food tempting.
  • The chai habit: tea always comes with something to eat.
  • Snacks sitting in plain sight at home or at your desk.

How to build a smart Indian evening snack

Use a simple formula: protein or fibre, plus flavour, in a small portion. A bowl of sprouts chaat has both. A handful of roasted chana with chai works too. The aim is a snack that holds you until dinner without making you overeat later.

Chai-time rules that work

  • Cut sugar a little at a time. India's dietary guidelines (ICMR-NIN, 2024) advise keeping added sugar low and limiting ultra-processed foods.
  • Swap biscuits and rusk for roasted chana or makhana.
  • Pour a small portion into a bowl or plate. Do not eat from the packet.
  • Sit down and eat slowly, away from the phone and TV.

10-minute Sunday snack prep

  1. RoastDry-roast chana and makhana. Store in airtight jars.
  2. SproutSoak moong or chana overnight, drain and let them sprout for a day.
  3. ChopCut cucumber and carrot sticks. Keep them in a box in the fridge.
  4. DipHang curd for a quick mint or garlic dip.
Sarita's tip 🌿

Put the snack in a bowl before 4 pm, not after you feel starving. Hunger makes every choice harder.

Easy swaps for family tea-time

  • Samosa or kachori→Dhokla or baked sabzi cutlet
  • Biscuits with chai→Roasted chana or makhana
  • Pakoda→Sprouts bhel or moong chilla
  • Namkeen and chips→Roasted peanuts or popcorn
  • Cold drink or juice→Chaas or nimbu pani with little sugar

Most of these work for the whole family, so you do not need to make a separate "diet" snack.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Trusting "healthy" packaged snacks like multigrain chips or bars without reading labels.
  • Eating nuts and dry fruit without measuring. They are healthy but calorie-dense.
  • Skipping snacks and then overeating at dinner.
  • Snacking from boredom or stress, not hunger.
  • Drinking extra calories, like sweet chai refills and juices.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best evening snack for weight loss?

The best snack has some protein and fibre, like roasted chana, sprouts chaat, makhana, dhokla, or curd with fruit. The right portion and a snack you enjoy matter more than any single food.

Is it okay to snack in the evening when trying to lose weight?

Yes. A planned snack can stop you from getting too hungry and overeating at dinner. What counts is the choice, the portion and your food across the whole day.

Can I have chai with my snack?

Yes. Reduce the sugar slowly, keep the cup size sensible, and pair it with roasted chana or makhana instead of biscuits or fried namkeen.

Are makhana and roasted chana really healthy?

They are good choices when dry-roasted or roasted with very little oil and eaten in a small handful. Packaged flavoured versions can be high in oil, salt and sugar, so check the label.

How many snacks should I have in a day?

Many women do well with one planned snack. Some need two, depending on meal timing, hunger and activity. Adjust based on how you feel and what your plan needs.

What can I eat if I am hungry after dinner?

Try a small bowl of curd, a fruit or a glass of chaas. If you are hungry every night, your dinner may need more protein and fibre.
